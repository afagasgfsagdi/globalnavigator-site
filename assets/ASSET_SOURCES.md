# Asset sources

- `plane.svg`, `ship.svg`: Font Awesome Free by Fonticons, Inc. Source: https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/7.x/svgs/solid. Icons licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
- `file-lines.svg`, `cube.svg`: Font Awesome Free by Fonticons, Inc. Source: https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/7.x/svgs/solid. Icons licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
- `earth-blue-marble.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble satellite/topography texture, distributed by three-globe. Source: https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe/tree/master/example/img. Credit: Reto Stöckli / NASA Earth Observatory (NASA public-domain imagery).
- `earth-at-night.jpg`: NASA/GSFC Earth-at-night city-light map, re-encoded from the 2048×1024 PNG for web delivery. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2916 and https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a002900/a002916/earthatnight-2048.png. Credit: Craig Mayhew and Robert Simmon (NASA/GSFC), DMSP-OLS data.

- `globe/ship-cargo-b.glb` and `globe/Textures/colormap.png`: Kenney Watercraft Kit, model served by Flockbay, CC0 1.0. Source: https://flockbay.com/assets/ship-cargo-b/. Used as a scaled, reoriented 3D globe marker.
- `globe/A350_nologo.glb`: amvlab Aircraft Models, CC BY 4.0. Source: https://github.com/amvlab/aircraft-models. Adapted by scaling, centering, and reorienting the aircraft for the animated globe route; no logo variant. Credit: amvlab. License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
- `globe/icesat-satellite.glb`: Low-poly web-optimized GLB of NASA/Ames Research Center's ICESat (A), 976 triangles; downloaded from models.codelove.in, whose catalog marks the satellite model public domain. Original NASA model: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/ice-clouds-and-land-elevation-satellite-icesat-a/. Model page: https://models.codelove.in/models/ice-clouds-and-land-elevation-satellite-icesat-a/. Used as an animated 3D satellite marker on the globe.
- `three-addons/loaders/GLTFLoader.js` and `three-addons/utils/BufferGeometryUtils.js`: Three.js r169 add-on modules, MIT License. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r169/examples/jsm.
- `three-addons/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js`: Meshoptimizer decoder module bundled with Three.js r169 examples, MIT License. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r169/examples/jsm/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js.
- `agent/RobotExpressive.glb`: RobotExpressive animated humanoid by Tomás Laulhé; model and converted GLB by Don McCurdy. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive. License: CC0 1.0. Used as the GlobalNavigator agent's animated 3D avatar.
